About the data

  • Saxo Ratios and final drive from MA/5N tooth counts and the Saxo handbook.
  • Polo Ratios from 085 gearbox code tables. The reverse ratio and the 3/4 + 5th synchro positions come from teardown reports, not a VW manual.
  • Corsa* The Corsa B workshop manual (So wird's gemacht, Band 90) confirms the F13 gearbox, engine data and gate, but doesn't cover gearbox internals, so the synchro layout is assumed.
  • GX3* Geely doesn't publish the S170B2's ratios or synchro layout, so those are estimates.
  • All cars Torque curves, weights and clutch/synchro capacities are approximations. Drawings are schematic.
